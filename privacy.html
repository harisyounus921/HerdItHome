Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date you publish this page]

Herd It Home is a farm game made by [your name or company name] ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses and why. We wrote it to be short and plain.

The short version

  • The game never asks for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location or contacts.
  • Players cannot type anything in the game. There is no chat and no messaging.
  • The game has no advertising and no advertising or tracking tools.
  • To show the leaderboard, the game sends a score and a random, anonymous player ID to an online service.

Information kept on your device

The game saves a few things on the phone or tablet so they are there next time you play:

  • your best score;
  • the animal picture and the ready-made name you chose (for example "Speedy Chicken 27");
  • your star total, and the world and animal looks you picked;
  • a score that has not been sent yet, if you played without an internet connection.

Deleting the game from the device removes this information.

Information sent online for the leaderboard

The leaderboard is run by Unity Gaming Services, a service provided by Unity Technologies. When the game is connected to the internet it sends:

  • An anonymous player ID. Unity creates this random ID the first time the game connects. It is not a name and it is not linked to an account, an email address or any other detail about you.
  • Your score and the time it was sent.
  • Your chosen animal picture and ready-made name, sent as three small numbers. The name is picked from a fixed list of friendly words, so it never contains a real name.

Like any online service, Unity's servers also receive the technical information needed to deliver a response over the internet, such as the device's IP address. We use the information above only to run the leaderboard. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

You can read how Unity handles information in the Unity Privacy Policy.

What other players can see

Other players can see your animal picture, your ready-made name, your score and your position on the leaderboard. Nothing else about you is shown.

Children

Herd It Home is suitable for children. The game is designed so that a child cannot share personal information: there is nothing to type, no chat, and no way to contact another player. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous player ID is used only to run the leaderboard.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, or want a leaderboard entry removed, please contact us using the details below.

Playing without the leaderboard

The game works without an internet connection. Offline, nothing is sent, and the game is fully playable.

How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • The weekly leaderboard starts fresh every Monday. The all-time leaderboard keeps each player's best score.
  • To have a leaderboard entry deleted, email us and include the Player ID shown at the bottom of the "Pick your player" screen in the game (tap the player name on the home screen to open it). We will delete the entry and reply to confirm.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, for example if a new feature is added, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
