Questions and answers

How do I play?

Cows, chickens and goats walk up three lanes. Swipe an animal left or right so it reaches its own pen. Tap an animal that is already in the right lane to make it dash home. Tap the lion and the snake to scare them away. Each round lasts 90 seconds.

What happens if the lion or snake gets into a pen?

The animals in that pen get a fright and run away (nobody is hurt). The pen's counter goes back to zero, the points for those animals are taken off, and the animals come back up the lanes so you can herd them home again.

How do I open new worlds and animal looks?

Every round gives you up to 3 stars. Your stars add up, and are never spent. New worlds open on the Scene button and new animal looks open in the Animals screen as your star total grows. There is nothing to buy.

Does the game have adverts or in-app purchases?

No. Herd It Home has no adverts and no in-app purchases.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. The whole game works offline. Only the leaderboard needs a connection; a score made offline is sent the next time the game is online.

How do I change my leaderboard name or picture?

On the home screen, tap your name at the top left. Pick an animal picture and shuffle for a new ready-made name. Players can never type a name, so no real names appear on the leaderboard.

Why is my leaderboard rank not showing?

The leaderboard needs an internet connection. Check that the device is online, then go back to the home screen. The weekly leaderboard starts fresh every Monday.

Will I lose my stars if I delete the game?

Stars, chosen worlds and looks are saved on the device. Deleting the game removes them, so a new install starts fresh.

How do I delete my leaderboard entry?

Tap your name on the home screen. At the bottom of that screen you will see your Player ID. Email it to us and we will delete your entry and reply to confirm.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later.

The game is slow or will not start. What can I do?
  • Close the game fully and open it again.
  • Restart the device.
  • Make sure the game is updated to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Still stuck? Email us with your device model and we will look into it.

For parents

Herd It Home is made for children. There is no chat, nothing to type and no way to contact other players. The game does not ask for any personal details. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.
