Delete your data

This page explains how to ask for the data of Herd It Home, made by Haris Younus, to be deleted.

Players never create an account in Herd It Home. The only data kept online is the player's leaderboard entry, which is linked to a random, anonymous Player ID.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Open Herd It Home and tap your player name at the top left of the home screen.
  2. At the bottom of that screen, find your Player ID and write it down.
  3. Email us at harisyounus921@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Player ID.

Email a deletion request

We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm. A parent or guardian can make the request for a child.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous Player ID
  • Your scores on the weekly and all-time leaderboards
  • Your chosen animal picture and ready-made name

What is kept

Nothing. We do not keep any of this data after deleting it.

Data saved on your device

Your best score, stars, chosen worlds and animal looks are saved only on your phone or tablet. To delete them, delete the game from the device.

If the game is opened again after your entry is deleted, it starts with a new anonymous Player ID.
